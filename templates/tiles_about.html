WHAT WE DO

A tile-led collection for homes and commercial spaces.

Our range brings together GVT, PGVT, full-body and double-charge tiles, including selected 5 mm slim full-body designs. From calm stone-inspired surfaces to more expressive textures, each catalogue offers different formats and finishes to explore.

We value quality, aesthetics and personal service. Our team can help you compare options and narrow the collection to suit your project; exact specifications and current stock are confirmed with each enquiry.

QUESTIONS WE HEAR OFTEN

Good to know before you visit.

Where is your tile showroom?

Our showroom is at Shop 13, Soni Tower CHSL, Haridas Nagar, Borivali (West), Mumbai 400092. Call or WhatsApp +91 84339 88032 before you visit and we will have samples ready.

Which brands do you sell?

We are an authorised dealer for Simpolo tiles and sanitaryware and for KOHLER bathware, and we also carry our own range of 5 mm slim tiles and full-body tiles.

Do you help work out how many tiles I need?

Yes. Every tile page has a box calculator, and our team can measure up from your drawings and suggest the right size, finish and quantity with extra for cutting.

Do you supply projects outside Mumbai?

Yes. We supply homes, offices, hotels and builders across Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai and the rest of Maharashtra and India.
